Why “generated from code” matters

Because it makes the licence simple and the provenance checkable. Nothing in these packs was recorded, sampled or produced by a generative audio model — so there is no third-party rights holder anywhere in the chain, and no training data embedded in the files. The source that produced every asset is in the download, which means you can audit it, change it, and rebuild the library yourself.
