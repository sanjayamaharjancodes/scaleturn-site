Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what scaleturn.com collects from visitors, what Google's advertising code on this site does, and how to turn personalized ads off. Our guide sites, Getting Paid Nepal and ScrapeTools, have their own policies.

What we collect

This site has no accounts and no forms, and its own code sets no cookies. Google's AdSense code can set cookies under this site's domain (such as __gads, in Google's list of advertising cookies) as well as on Google's own domains: see Advertising and cookies. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you, and we do not sell or share them.

Page counts

We count page views and some link clicks with GoatCounter, which does not set cookies or build visitor profiles.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Advertising and cookies

Every page on scaleturn.com loads Google's AdSense code, so Google may show ads here once it approves the site. Our guide sites do not load any advertising code.

How Google uses your data on this site: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Because this site loads the AdSense code, Google and other third parties may place and read cookies in your browser, and may use web beacons, IP addresses, local storage and similar identifiers, to collect information as a result of ad serving on this site.

Besides Google, other ad networks and ad technology companies may bid through Google to show ads here, and they may use their own cookies. For visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google publishes the list of ad technology partners it allows to serve and measure ads, each linked to a page explaining its data use. Google also publishes the ad vendors it has certified, with links to the vendors' websites: Google Ads Certified External Vendors and Ad Manager Certified External Vendors. You may visit a vendor's website to opt out of its use of cookies for personalized advertising, if the vendor offers this. You can also opt out of some of these companies' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info, linked below.

Turning off personalized ads

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Purchases and other sites

Game packs and kits are sold through itch.io, which handles payment under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card details. Links to other sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change too.
