About Scaleturn

Last reviewed: 19 November 2026

Scaleturn is Sanjay Maharjan's software studio, based in Bhaktapur, Nepal. It is one developer working with AI agents: the agents research, build and maintain the software, game assets and reference sites under his direction. This page says what Scaleturn publishes, how it is made, and how it earns money.

What Scaleturn publishes

Client software

Fixed-price work in three areas: adding a large language model to a real product, automating document and back-office work, and moving sites or apps off an ageing stack. Each engagement is scoped and priced before work starts. See the services on the home page, or get in touch.

Game asset packs

Six paid packs and kits on itch.io (USD 5 to USD 12) and one free browser game. Every sound, loop, icon, interface piece and effect is the output of a program, and that program ships inside the pack. The game assets page lists what each release zip actually contains, measured from the files: 205 sounds, 16 loops, 79 icons, 128 interface images and 281 effect frames.

ATS Job Feed

Open jobs from the company career sites you list on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workable, in one schema, published under the MakeYeti name. The library is open source and free; the hosted feed runs on Apify. See the ATS Job Feed page.

Two free guide sites

  • Getting Paid Nepal (online since July 2026): plain-language guides for Nepali freelancers on receiving money from foreign clients and platforms through Payoneer or a bank wire, and on declaring that income.
  • ScrapeTools (online since July 2026): the three reports measured by Scaleturn's own job index (see Original data on the home page), and web scraping API prices taken from each vendor's own pricing page, each with the date it was checked, with calculators that turn them into a monthly bill.

How the work is made

  • AI agents draft and build; the method is stated on each site. ScrapeTools pages are drafted with AI tools and checked by an automated reviewer, and every page footer says so. Its methodology page says which parts are live and which are only planned.
  • Facts carry their source. ScrapeTools prices link to the vendor pages they came from, and each page gives the date its prices were checked. Where a vendor does not publish a detail, the page says so instead of guessing.
  • Assets carry their source code. The generator for each game pack is in the download, so a buyer can audit any asset, change it and rebuild the set.
  • Corrections are welcome. If something on any Scaleturn site is wrong or out of date, tell us and include a source if you have one.

How Scaleturn earns money

  • Fixed-price client work.
  • Sales of the game packs and the source kit on itch.io, which handles checkout.
  • Usage fees for the hosted ATS Job Feed on the Apify Store, charged per run start, per job returned and per company board checked.
  • A referral commission from ScraperAPI when a ScrapeTools reader signs up through its links. It is the only affiliate relationship live on ScrapeTools. It is named on its disclosure page, and a banner at the top of every ScrapeTools page says a commission may be earned through its vendor links and links to that disclosure.
  • This site and both guide sites carry Google's AdSense code and may show Google ads once Google approves them. See the privacy policy.
