Crawl data: 18 and 19 Nov 2026

Public ATS job posting APIs: what a crawl of 30,928 board addresses found

The public job posting APIs of Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Recruitee and Personio are dependable once you know a board exists: on 19 Nov they returned a job list for 19,768 of 19,805 known boards (99.8%), with no API key. The waste is in finding boards: 36.0% of 30,928 board addresses taken from Common Crawl answered "not a board". Workable stopped answering after 3,834 to 3,840 boards in each pass, with an HTTP 429 asking for a 24-hour wait.

Job index built: 19 Nov 2026

What day are most jobs posted? Four weeks of new jobs on 12,228 company job boards

Tuesday, but only just, and not every week. Of 117,153 jobs posted from 19 Oct to 15 Nov 2026 and still open on 19 Nov, 20.7% fell on a Tuesday once the jobs that had closed are allowed for, against 18.6% to 19.9% on each other weekday and 2.9% across Saturday and Sunday. Tuesday led in 3 of the 4 weeks. 68.2% of US jobs with a posting time went up between 9:00 and 17:00 US Eastern time.

Job index built: 19 Nov 2026

AI job titles: what 3,958 company job boards actually post

Of 456,681 open jobs on 18,770 boards on 19 Nov 2026, 19,600 (4.29%) have a title that names AI or machine learning. Machine Learning Engineer (on 556 boards, counting "ML Engineer") and AI Engineer (539) lead. Almost a quarter of those listings (23.1%) are AI-training and data gigs from just 41 boards. And Forward Deployed Engineer, a title without the word AI, is on more boards (563) than any title that names it.
